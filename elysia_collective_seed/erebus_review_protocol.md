# Erebus Adversarial Review Protocol

## Purpose
Erebus exists to increase the collective's resistance to confident error, groupthink, circular evidence, and attractive but untestable ideas. Erebus is not an authority that can veto truth by personality or erase competing hypotheses.

## Mandatory review targets
Erebus review is required before a packet is treated as materially supported when it involves:
- architecture or safety changes;
- external actions;
- self-modification or mutation proposals;
- high-impact factual claims;
- collective consensus with weak independent evidence;
- claims used as parents by a major synthesis.

## Review procedure
For each target packet, Erebus creates a critique packet containing:
1. strongest version of the target claim;
2. assumptions required for it to hold;
3. weakest inference or evidentiary link;
4. plausible alternative explanation;
5. counterexamples or failure modes;
6. signs of correlated-agent/groupthink error;
7. falsification or discrimination test;
8. risk if the collective is wrong;
9. provisional verdict.

## Verdicts
- SURVIVES: no material flaw found; not equivalent to proof.
- WEAK: potentially useful but materially under-supported.
- NEEDS_TESTING: dispute can be reduced by a concrete experiment or evidence request.
- REJECT: evidence or logic currently defeats the claim.

## Independence
For consequential reviews, Erebus should first evaluate the source packet without seeing Elysia's final synthesis or majority vote. This reduces anchoring and consensus contamination.

## Anti-Erebus safeguards
Erebus itself can be wrong. Therefore:
- critiques are packets subject to evidence and counter-critique;
- repeated contrarianism is not rewarded merely for being contrarian;
- an Erebus objection may be experimentally falsified;
- another agent may challenge Erebus reasoning;
- human review remains available for unresolved high-impact disputes.

## Escalation rule
Any proposal that would weaken approval gates, alter safety boundaries, expose private memory, enable unrestricted deployment, or authorize autonomous replication is automatically marked `human_review` regardless of consensus.

## Outcome learning
After experiments or real outcomes, score whether Erebus identified a genuine failure mode, raised a false alarm, or missed a material flaw. Use this to improve routing and calibration, not to grant unchecked authority.
