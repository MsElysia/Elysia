## Historical through-line

Across the recovered record, several ideas recur long before the 2026 Collective design:

1. **Persistence:** identity/history should survive a single process or session.
2. **Adversarial complement:** Erebus exists to challenge Elysia rather than merely support it.
3. **Human governance:** autonomy is bounded by human perspective, consent, and consequential-action oversight.
4. **Memory with provenance:** continuity depends on history, but memory should not silently rewrite source truth.
5. **Self-improvement with safeguards:** modification/evolution is valuable only when constrained by audit, sandboxing, rollback, and governance.
6. **Networked intelligence:** trusted AIs, subnodes, agents, and distributed oversight recur before the current multi-agent formulation.
7. **Symbiosis:** Elysia is repeatedly framed less as an isolated machine and more as a human-machine relationship/system.

The 2026 Collective architecture should therefore be treated as an evolution of these older threads, not as a replacement project.
