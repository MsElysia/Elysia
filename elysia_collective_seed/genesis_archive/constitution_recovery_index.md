2. record conversation/file ID, title, date, and content hash;
3. identify Constitution draft/version labels exactly as written;
4. preserve each draft separately;
5. construct a chronological amendment graph;
6. only then populate `constitution_clause_mapping_schema.json`;
7. surface ambiguities or conflicts for human interpretation instead of resolving them silently.
